The main backbone of video slot is reel which decides the overall feel of the game. A reel has multiple cyclic strips , 
each with L length of symbols .Number of strips decides the size of actual same windows where as
Frequency and position of symbols decides how big and how frequent a winning can be. 
During spin, game stops at a random symbols ,selecting the symbol and N symbols below it. 
That is how game window is formed (reel strips count X N symbols from stop)

In our case we have a reel with 5 reel strips each strips with 50 length i.e total of 50^5 combinations. 
We have picked two symbols after the reel stop position making total of 3 and window size of (5*3). 
Changing even a single symbol on reel strip alters every window i.e 3 of 50 stops , so small alter can have major shift on the stats of the game.

How a spin pays : Bet entered by player is the total bet, which is divided into each paylines (in our case 20 payline) , 
hence line bet is totalbet/20. After formation of result the symbols are matched 
against the payline pattern which can be straight , V shape or any shape that are predefined and clearly communicated to users . 
Continuos occurance of 3 or more of any single symbols on any of the payline pays the winning as per the paytable defined , 
paytable amount is multiplied by lines bet. There are special symbols wild and scatter , wild can subtitute for any symbols 
and has its on pay too. In case if sequence of wild and sequence of symbols with wild both completes the payline  ,higher is rewarded. 
Scatter is not compared against payline instead it is paid on any occurance on entire window, even dublicate occurance on same reel strip. 
Scatter trigger a free spins where N (10 in our case) spins are rewarded to player without any extra pay.

Free game : Free games are triggered by occurance of 3 or more scatter anywhere on the window. 
Scatter can appear again inside the free spins rewarding additional 10 free spins hence a re trigger. 
So number of free spins is not fixed number. Free game is played with separate reelset , so mathematicians has to manage two reel  , base and free reel.

Stats whose long run average has to be maintained by mathematicians
Return to player (RTP) : Return to player is average amount paid back to player , 
for example a game with rtp of 95% pay back 95 dollar for every 100 dollar spent by user on average. 
Total rtp consists of base game rtp and free game rtp. Our game has target of 0.95 (0.57 base and 0.38 free).

Hit rate : Hitrate decides how frequent wins are rewarded. Hitrate is quoted as 1 in X i.e 
In every X spins player has won some amount of winning. since rtp is maintained regardless of the hitrate , 
hence higher hitrate gives less frequent win but bigger wins (termed as high volatile game) ,
lower hitrate gives frequent lower wins (considered low volatile games). Hitrate is calculated seprately on free game.

Trigger rate : It is number of base spins per free spin trigger . In case of free game its number of freespins per retrigger. 
This is also quoted as 1 in X spins.

Slot mathematicians prepare a mathsheet which consist of reelset and a parsheet .Sheets also have game descriptions defining game 
rules along with paytable , paylines. Parsheet has game stats such as rtp hitrate , trigger rate win distribution max win cap. 
RTP is the number regulators check and a same game can be released with different rtp each with different reelset . 

Reelset are tuned (changing frequency and position of symbols on each reel strip) manually by mathematicians. 
An extra wild can raise rtp and hitrate. An extra scatter can decrease free spin trigger rate , highly fluctuating free winnings and overall rtp . 
In practice mathematicians change strips , run several millions simulation check result and redo whole process untill desired result is achieved. 
The rest of the paper ask how well this loop can be handed to a search algorithm and which algorithm and which algorithm should run it.
