Genetic algorithm 
THe ga starts with 10 random parents (keep same parents for all combinations of ga and for 
baselines too) and evaulated once. In each generation parents are selected , children
are created from selected parents , children's reelset are mutated
evaluated and then decided which of the parents can be replaced. GA keeps track of 
best reelset it has seen so far , since combination such as generatinal scheme 
can lose its best child.
Selection : There mainly three method used for the selection of parents in our project.
On all three operator we only consider the ranking of population exluding the 
noise size. Ranking was choosen because the score F are in range 80-100 on beginingn
and falls down to 0.1 during end. So fitness propotional selection will work 
very differently during start and end.
Tornament selection(k=3). For this 3 distintn random parents are drawn and best 
among them is selected .Single parent comes from single draw.
Roulette selection : Each parents are ranked using baker's linear ranking formula 
(wρ = (2−s)+2(s−1)(ρ−1)/(μ−1),)with selection presure s =2. Rank 1 is worst rank and u is best rank . Best rank 
always gets weight of 2 where as worst 0 so worst is never chosen. For rouletter to 
single parents is chosen during a single spin.
SUS - Uses same baker's linear ranking method , however instead weight being 
selection chance , a pointer is place at random start and each parents gets slice
on the scale. Pointer starting position is based on number of parents to be 
selection and number of pointers got by parents slice is number of times 
that specific parents got selected.
So even though roulette and sus have same expected selection count , routelle 
does have of best parent not being selected at all where as in sus best always 
gets selected. This matter on generational replacement as roulette can lose 
best gene where steady state protects the best genes so its not affected.

Replacement: there are 3 method ranging from replacing all the parents to replacing 
a single worst parents. Mutation applied on each child that are create where 
mutation percentage u means replacement of u number of symbols from total 
symbols count of 250 (5 reelstrip * 50 on each reels)
Stead state: In this method 2 parents are selected using above mentioned selection 
method and a single child is produced , the child replaces the the worst parents
if it better than it. Hence best child is always saved.
Generational : 10 parents are selected and they are paired togheter  , each pair
creates a child with total of 10 children. 10 children replaces all of the parents
Hence best gene can be lost if its not passed on.
Elitist generation: In this method 2 best parents are copied as it is where are 8 
other parents are replaced by 8 children created by pairing of remaining 8 parents.

baselines
Random search draws a new random parent in each generation using same method that
was used to generate initial 10 parents. No mutation is used during random search 
and only the best parent is kept. It shows how seer luck (pure rng) is not enough
to tune the slot.

Hill climbing : It is (1+1 ) Ea . It selects the best parents among the initial parents
mutate the best one and replace it if its better than the best . hence for hill 
climing current one is the best one. Hill climing is same as steady state ga with 
population of 1 and no crossover. That makes it main baseline ga has to beat. 
Beating hill climbing proves crossover and mutation has any advantage on the tuning.

Simulated annealing: It is same as hill climing hover it has (-delta/r) , where 
"delta formula" is the probablity of worst child being selected. Log scale is used 
because score varies from 100s at the beginingn to 0.1s during end improving by 
3 folds during the run . linear scale would either selecy far too much at end or 
almost none at begining. The temperature falls geometrically
from τ0 = 0.14 at the first evaluation to τend = 0.002 at the last, and the method
returns the best reel set it has seen.
Both hill climbing and simulated annealing start from the best member of
the GA’s initial population.
