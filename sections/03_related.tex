\paragraph{Game balancing and content generation.} Search-based procedural content generation uses search methods, such as evolution, to create game content, and a fitness function scores each candidate~\cite{togelius2011search}. Game balancing uses the same idea on an existing game. Morosan and Poli~\cite{morosan2017automated} balanced Ms PacMan and StarCraft with evolutionary algorithms. In our problem we have asked whether a GA, compared against local search, is the best to achieve a set of targets.

\paragraph{Optimisation under noise.} Noisy fitness has been studied widely~\cite{jin2005evolutionary,rakshit2017noisy}. The simplest fix is resampling, i.e.\ evaluating a candidate several times and taking the average, but this uses up evaluations. Fitzpatrick and Grefenstette~\cite{fitzpatrick1988genetic} found that a GA can gain more from evaluating more candidates than from averaging more samples. Arnold and Beyer~\cite{arnold2002local} showed for the (1+1) evolution strategy that a parent that is not re-evaluated gets overvalued, hence its children succeed less often, although performance can still improve. We have also measured this optimism in our project, for which we re-evaluated the best reel set of every run on a new seed with $10^8$ spins. As a result, the base-stage median error came out higher than the in-run median in 36 of 44 method--mutation combinations.

\paragraph{Slot machine mathematics.} Gambling research describes how slot games are designed: Harrigan and Dixon analysed PAR sheets, the documents that list a game's reel strips and prizes~\cite{harrigan2009par}, and studied versions of one game with different RTPs~\cite{harrigan2010tight}. Reel strips have also been tuned by search. Balabanov et al.~\cite{balabanov2015slot} used a GA to reach a desired RTP by controlling the symbol distribution, Keremedchiev et al.~\cite{keremedchiev2017slot} used an evolutionary algorithm on base-game RTP, and Kamanas et al.~\cite{kamanas2021slot} used variable neighbourhood search on the base game. Even though these studies targeted RTP, we have targeted hit rate and trigger rate (retrigger rate in the free game) along with RTP on the base and free game stages.

\paragraph{Steady-state and generational GAs.} GENITOR~\cite{whitley1989genitor} replaces one member at a time, whereas a generational GA replaces the whole population. Syswerda~\cite{syswerda1991study} compared the two, and De Jong and Sarma~\cite{dejong1993generation} studied the share of the population replaced at each step. Goldberg and Deb~\cite{goldberg1991comparative} compared selection schemes by takeover time, i.e.\ the number of generations selection alone needs to fill the population with copies of the best member. Rogers and Pr\"ugel-Bennett~\cite{rogers1999genetic} showed that genetic drift is twice as fast under steady-state selection as under generational selection. We have tested these replacement schemes under noisy evaluations, with $\mu = 10$ and four mutation strengths, and used hill climbing and simulated annealing as baselines to see when a GA's population pays off.
